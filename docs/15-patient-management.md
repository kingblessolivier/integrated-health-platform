# 15 — Patient Management

## Registration & identity

- **NIDA integration** auto-populates verified demographics at every registration — no
  manual re-typing.
- A **globally unique patient identifier** links all encounters across facilities.
- A **master patient index** detects and resolves duplicate records automatically.
- For unregistered patients (newborns, emergencies, refugees), a **temporary identity** is
  created and later reconciled.

## Longitudinal record

- Every visit, diagnosis, prescription, lab result, imaging report, and procedure attaches
  to **one record**.
- Community health worker interactions and village-level assessments appear in the timeline.
- Allergies, chronic conditions, and surgical history are prominently displayed and
  **checked against every order**.

## Appointments & referrals

- Patients can book via the app, or be booked by a clinician.
- Referrals carry the **full clinical context**; the receiving facility sees it before the
  patient arrives.
- The mutuelle **transfer-note** requirement is embedded in the referral workflow for tiered
  facilities.

## Consent & privacy

- Patients can grant or withdraw consent for specific actors to access their record.
- Consent is enforced **in addition to** role-based authorisation.
- All consent changes are recorded in the **audit chain**.

## Chronic / NCD care

- Care plans with refill schedules and review dates are set for long-term conditions.
- Missed refills and overdue reviews surface as **alerts** to the treating clinician.
- Trend charts across visits track deterioration or improvement.
