# 18 — Workforce & HR Management

## Onboarding & verification

- Every new staff member is onboarded using their **National ID**; NIDA verifies identity.
- The **professional licence** is checked against the relevant national council registry in
  real time.
- A digital employment contract aligned to **Rwanda Labour Law N° 017/2020** is generated,
  signed with a cryptographic key, and sealed with a **verification QR code**.

## Access provisioning

- On hiring, the system provisions the **exact role, facility, and data scope** — no more,
  no less.
- On transfer, access to the old branch is revoked and the new branch granted at the
  confirmed time, automatically.
- On resignation or termination, **all tokens are invalidated immediately**.

## Payroll & statutory deductions

- Monthly payroll is computed from attendance and shift logs.
- **PAYE** income tax is calculated against RRA brackets automatically.
- **RSSB** pension and social-security contributions are deducted and flagged for remittance.
- Payslips are published to the staff self-service portal.

## Professional compliance

- Licence-expiry dates are tracked; alerts fire 90, 60, and 30 days before expiry.
- A staff member whose licence lapses **cannot be assigned clinical duties** until renewed.
- Mandatory training completion is tracked per staff type.
