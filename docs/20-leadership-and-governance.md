# 20 — Leadership & Governance

## Facility leadership

- The facility director sees whole-facility KPIs: bed occupancy, throughput, revenue,
  insurance receivables, staff coverage, stock exposure, quality indicators, and HMIS
  compliance.
- Department heads see their unit's queue, resource levels, and staff.
- **Drill-down** lets a director investigate an anomaly without leaving the dashboard.

## District governance

- The **DHO** has a performance league table of all facilities against national targets.
- The **district epidemiologist** has a real-time disease map and the authority to trigger
  outbreak response from the platform.
- The **CBHI manager** tracks the district mutuelle fund as a live balance sheet.

## National governance

- The **MoH** dashboard shows the full country: health indicators, supply chain,
  infrastructure, financial flows, data quality.
- **Rwanda FDA** monitors medicine quality and safety from the same data stream.
- **RSSB** tracks claim-settlement performance and fund sustainability.

## Performance-based financing (PBF)

- CHW cooperatives are scored monthly on **timeliness, diagnostic accuracy, and stockout
  records**.
- Facility-level PBF indicators are computed automatically from care events.
- Payment is triggered from within the platform, routed to cooperative accounts.
